How to Play Lumina

1

Laser Emitters: Emit a continuous beam of colored light across the grid.

2

Mirrors: Click any mirror tile to rotate it 90 degrees and change the reflection path.

3

Splitters & Targets: Splitters divide a beam into two directions. Targets must be fully illuminated to win.

4

Sandbox Mode: Build your own custom laser puzzles, place elements, and test them out!
